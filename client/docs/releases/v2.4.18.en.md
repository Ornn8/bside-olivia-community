# 2.4.18

Covers and original songs that are made overnight no longer show up as failed.

## Improvements

- **Queued songs no longer report a failure**: Covers and original songs that are produced in the overnight run now show "Queued for tonight's production run" in the letter and arrive on their own when they're done. If you shut down your computer in the meantime, the app picks up the same song the next time you open it instead of starting over.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
