# 2.5.16

She recalls past conversations more accurately: they are now found by retrieval and then refined, instead of being picked by a single judgment call.

## Improvements

- **Recalling past conversations now uses retrieval and re-ranking**: when she remembers something you talked about before, she first finds the most relevant passages by content and then keeps the ones that really match, instead of asking a model to decide which to bring up. Replies are faster, and nothing is forced in when nothing relevant is found.
- **Automatic fallback**: if this step is temporarily unavailable, the former way of choosing is used and the conversation is not affected.
- **It can be turned off**: set the environment variable OLIVIA_R3_RECALL=0, or put {"enabled": false} in r3-recall.json in the data folder, to go back to the former way.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
