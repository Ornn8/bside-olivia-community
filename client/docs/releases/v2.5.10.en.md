# 2.5.10

Diaries and monthly stories are less likely to get lost.

## Improvements

- **Diaries and monthly stories retry with a different model**: when a model cannot write the diary or monthly story, the reason is kept and the next try switches to another model instead of repeating the same one.
- **Replies in other shapes are accepted as diaries**: when the model puts the diary in a code block, adds a few sentences around it, or leaves a ragged ending, a genuine diary is still recovered as the entry. A clear refusal is never taken for a diary.
- **Why a diary could not be written shows up in diagnostics**: the reason, the model used and the reply size are recorded, never the diary text.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
