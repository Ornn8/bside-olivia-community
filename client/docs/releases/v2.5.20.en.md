# 2.5.20

Letters she starts can carry a photo, things she promised are marked done once delivered, calls follow the English interface, sung voice messages longer than a minute are heard and sung back, and the window no longer stays black on laptops running the app on the integrated GPU.

## Improvements

- **Letters she starts can carry a photo**: before writing, she decides whether the letter should come with a photo of herself at that moment. If so, the letter mentions it naturally and goes out together with the photo once it is ready. If the photo cannot be made or takes too long, the letter is rewritten as plain text, so a letter never talks about a photo that is not there.
- **Things she promised are marked done once delivered**: when she has sent the photo, song, cover, piano recording or video she promised, the matching item in "Planned" is marked completed instead of hanging there and later turning into "Old item, progress unconfirmed". Earlier deliveries are checked once after you update.
- **Calls follow the English interface**: with the English interface, she answers in English in a call and speaks English.
- **Sung voice messages longer than a minute are heard and sung back**: before, a voice message over a minute was treated as "could not hear". Now one of up to five minutes is heard in pieces cut at quiet places, and she knows its total length and what each piece held. The whole recording is kept, and when she sings it back she sings all of it. If the whole recording cannot be sung, she sings its clearest part and tells you which seconds.
- **Black window on laptops using the integrated GPU**: when Olivia is set to "Power saving" in Windows graphics settings, the window could stay black. Now each start makes the two drawing programs use the same GPU, and if the window still never paints, the app restarts once in a compatible way. "Settings → Updates and help" has a new "Compatibility display mode": Auto / On / Off, effective from the next start.
- **The startup animation no longer hangs around**: it closes by itself at most 30 seconds after the video ends (60 seconds after it starts), and a click skips it.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
