# 2.5.4

Videos and songs in her letters now open, and a network drop while a song is being made no longer shows it as failed.

## Fixes

- **Videos in her letters play**: when a letter she writes to you carries a video (such as a song video that was finished later), the letter now shows the video; before, only its words showed. Letters you already received show it after the update when you open them again.
- **Her letters can carry a finished song**: a song that did not reach you before is sent with one of her letters once it is ready, plays right on the letter, and can be added to your library.
- **Waiting for songs and videos survives a network drop**: some songs and song videos are made at night. If the PC loses the network or sleeps for a while in between, the letter no longer says the voice or video could not be made; it waits until it is ready.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
