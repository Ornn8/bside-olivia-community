# 2.5.24

Letters she writes on her own now have a Download button too.

## Fixes

- **Her own letters can be downloaded**: the header of a letter she wrote on her own had only the title and the time. The Download button is back; one click saves the letter as a picture, and a letter with a video saves the picture as well.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
