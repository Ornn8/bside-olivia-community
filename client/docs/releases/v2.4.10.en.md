# 2.4.10

The names you call each other are more accurate.

## Fixes

- **Names you call each other**: Everyday replies such as "OK then" and self-descriptions such as "I'm a baseball player" are no longer taken as names you call each other. Names recorded wrongly before are corrected within a day of updating, and she also picks up the names you really use from your recent chats.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
