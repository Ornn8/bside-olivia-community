# 2.5.12

In QQ you can ask her to cover a song you share; things missed in earlier QQ chats are looked at again in the background and added to memory.

## New

- **Share a song with her in QQ and ask her to sing it, and she covers it for you**: send her a NetEase Cloud Music or Bilibili song card or link and say you want to hear her sing it. She sings her own cover of it and sends it back to you.

## Improvements

- **QQ chats: lines missed earlier are reviewed again in the background and added to memory**: things in your QQ chats that were not remembered before are looked at once more in the background, and the ones worth keeping are added to memory.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
