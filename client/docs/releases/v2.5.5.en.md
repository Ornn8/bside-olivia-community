# 2.5.5

Her songs can be added to your library again, and a letter with a video keeps her words.

## Fixes

- **Her songs can be added to your library**: "Add to library" on a song in her letter used to say it failed; it works now. Songs that did not get added before can be added after the update.
- **A letter with a video keeps her words**: when a letter she writes to you carries a video, her words, signature and time now show under the video; before, only the video showed.
- **No stray tags at the start of letters and replies**: now and then a letter or reply began with a bracketed line like `[语音回复 {...}]`, which was also read aloud. Such lines are removed now; QQ faces and ordinary brackets are left alone.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
