# 2.5.0

On QQ she feels more like a real person: she uses QQ faces, pokes you, posts on QQ Space and watches Bilibili videos with you, and she answers faster. Letters can now carry pictures for her to see.

## New

- **QQ faces and pokes**: she understands the QQ faces, mall stickers and share cards you send, answers with QQ faces of her own, and sometimes pokes you before she speaks. Poke her and she reacts.
- **QQ status and QQ Space**: her QQ status follows what she is doing. In the evening she posts on QQ Space with a photo she sent you that day and changes her signature. She likes and comments on your posts, and answers your comments under hers. Each part can be turned off on its own in the QQ section of Settings.
- **Bilibili together**: send her a Bilibili link or share card and she watches and listens to the video before talking about it.
- **Dice and rock-paper-scissors**: roll a die or throw a hand on QQ and she rolls or throws back, and knows who won.
- **She draws**: now and then she sends you a little doodle, on QQ or with a letter. You can also just ask her to draw.
- **Pictures in letters**: an ordinary letter can carry up to 4 pictures (PNG, JPG or WebP). Use "Add pictures" or paste them into the letter. She answers with the pictures in mind, and they lie under your paper like the photo she sends with hers.
- **Pick her outfit**: outfits you own in the wardrobe now have "Wear this". She wears that outfit today, in her photos too. Choose a style again to let her pick her own outfits.
- **New model**: DeepSeek Flash is in the model list.

## Improvements

- **Faster replies**: her QQ voice starts while the reply is being checked, and connections to the server are reused, so you wait noticeably less.
- **Photos she means**: once a photo has been sent she no longer says she is still taking it, and she sends photos only when they fit.
- **Late-night chats**: message her in the middle of the night and she wakes up to talk for a bit; night photos are taken at home.
- **Song letters say when**: when you ask her to cover or sing a song, her letter tells you roughly when you will hear it, and she no longer says she has never heard the song you attached.
- **Wardrobe pictures**: the wardrobe page uses small compressed pictures, so it opens faster.
- **Her answer to a gift**: when you give her something, her answer goes out in the next pause of your chat instead of waiting until you stop writing. If QQ never pauses, it arrives as a letter.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
