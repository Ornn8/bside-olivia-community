# 2.4.17

She now suggests playing for you or calling at the right moment; she repeats herself less and gets the timing of old memories right.

## New

- **She makes suggestions**: When you chat on QQ and you're relaxing, missing her or wishing you could hear her voice, she may ask whether you'd like her to play a piece for you, or to call. She only plays if you say yes, and you can reach her by tapping "Call" in the app. She brings each one up at most once every few days, and never while you're busy or upset.

## Improvements

- **The right timing for old memories**: When she recalls earlier letters and chats, she goes by how long ago they really were and no longer calls something from weeks ago "yesterday".
- **Less repetition**: She notices the phrases she's been leaning on lately and says things in new ways.
- **Consistent "you"**: When she's talking to you, her descriptions of what she does always address you as "you".
- **Her own titles for her letters**: Letters she writes to you on her own are titled after what they say, instead of all being called "Wanted to talk with you".

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
