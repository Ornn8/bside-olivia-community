# 2.4.11

Steadier startup, letters send normally after a deletion, video replies keep her letter, piano demos are on the library page, and missed diary entries are written.

## Fixes

- **Repairs itself when it won't open**: After Olivia has been run as administrator, some files could become readable by administrators only, and opening it normally would then fail. Opening it as administrator now restores the files' permissions, and if a normal start fails for this reason, Windows asks once for administrator approval — choose Yes and Olivia repairs itself and opens again.
- **Clearer startup records**: When a start fails, the log now notes which component was missing and whether it was a permission problem, which makes it easier to sort out.
- **Letters send again after a deletion**: After deleting a letter whose reply had a voice message or photo, the next letters and QQ messages could fail to get a reply until she had sent a few more voice messages or photos. Deleting a letter no longer causes this, and records left by earlier deletions are skipped.
- **Video replies keep her letter**: When she replied with a video (a cover, a song or herself speaking), the letter she wrote could no longer be read once the video arrived. Her letter now follows the video.
- **Piano demos can be found**: The library page now has a "Piano demo" button next to "Import local performances": upload a MIDI for her to play, then listen to and download the recording. This entry had never been shown.
- **Missed diary entries are written**: Some diary entries of the last few days were not written because the model's reply came back in the wrong format, and once the tries ran out those days were not tried again. After updating, those days are written once more.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
