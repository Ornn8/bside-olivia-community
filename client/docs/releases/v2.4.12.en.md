# 2.4.12

Better nicknames; the world page no longer floods with repeats and activities last a plausible time; bedtime stories, soothing and piano are steadier; letters send again after an interrupted deletion; Import MIDI is easier to find.

## Fixes

- **Better nicknames**: Once a day she reads your recent conversation and notes how you two usually address each other, so the way she calls you in letters and QQ follows your habits.
- **No more repeats on the world page**: When she keeps doing the same thing, the world page groups those records and shows only the latest note.
- **Activities last a plausible time**: She no longer spends hours on one small thing, such as practising one passage; she moves on after a while.
- **Bedtime stories, soothing and piano are steadier**: On a poor connection, a bedtime story, soothing or piano piece she offered on QQ could turn into an ordinary voice message. She now keeps the last confirmed result, so one failed check no longer takes them away.
- **Letters send again after an interrupted deletion**: If Olivia was closed or failed while deleting a letter, later letters could stay stuck. An unfinished deletion no longer holds new letters back; at most they wait 10 minutes.
- **Import MIDI is easier to find**: The piano entry on the library page is now called "Import MIDI" and shows every time the library page opens.
- **Fuller feedback bundles**: When a start fails, the feedback bundle now carries the launcher's error code, which makes it easier to sort out.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
