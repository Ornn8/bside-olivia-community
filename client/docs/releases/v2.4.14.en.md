# 2.4.14

She recalls what you talked about more accurately, and replies sooner.

## Improvements

- **Better recall of earlier chats**: When she thinks back to what you said and the little things she noted, she now understands them in a new way and finds the chats that really relate to your message.
- **Faster replies**: The wait while she thinks back before each reply drops from a second or two to a fraction of a second, and no longer stalls for a few seconds now and then.
- **A one-time tidy-up after the update**: After updating she goes through your earlier chats again in the background, usually in under an hour. She still remembers everything meanwhile.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
