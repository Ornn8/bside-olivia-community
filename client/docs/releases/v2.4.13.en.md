# 2.4.13

The diary no longer mixes the two of you up; new "Rewrite this entry".

## Fixes

- **The diary no longer mixes you up**: Some entries were written from your side, giving you her name, her cat and the photos she sent you, and one such entry could lead the next days astray. She now keeps apart what you said and what she said, checks the entry once more when it is written, and writes it again if it came out the wrong way round.
- **Remembered details stay the right way round**: Small things like who calls whom what are noted by who actually said them, and ones already noted the wrong way round are corrected.
- **New "Rewrite this entry"**: Open an entry, choose "Rewrite this entry" and confirm, and she writes that day again from its letters and chats. The new entry replaces the old one; your comments stay.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
