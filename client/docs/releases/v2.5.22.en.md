# 2.5.22

Sending a letter with a picture, or as a voice or video reply, no longer stops to ask for a second confirmation.

## Improvements

- **One less confirmation when sending a letter**: when a letter has a picture attached, or goes out as a voice or video reply, pressing send no longer pops up a second "send?" question; it just goes out.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
