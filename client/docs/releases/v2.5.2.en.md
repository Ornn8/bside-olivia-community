# 2.5.2

A song video that could not be finished in time now arrives with one of her letters once it is made.

## Improvements

- **A finished video arrives with her letter**: if a song video you asked for could not be made at the time, she writes you a letter when it is ready and attaches the video, so you can watch it right in the letter.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
