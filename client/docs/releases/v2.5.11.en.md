# 2.5.11

Replies to voice and video letters are more reliable.

## Improvements

- **Voice and video letters: a reply split into several parts is shown and spoken as one complete letter**: when the model returns the reply in several parts, it is displayed and read aloud as the full letter, with no raw formatting showing.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
