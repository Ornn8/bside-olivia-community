# 2.5.3

She now looks things up on the web when you need it, and a backup now carries her diary and memories too.

## Improvements

- **She looks it up when it matters**: when you ask about the weather, the news or whether something is true, she checks the web first and tells you in her own words; everyday chat and letters about daily life are not looked up. Only the part of your message that can be shared publicly is used for the lookup, never anything private between you.
- **Backups carry the diary and memories**: "Export backup" in Settings now saves her diary (with your comments), the long-term memories she keeps and the text of voice calls; import the file on a new PC and the diary and memories carry on, without overwriting diary entries already there. Very long chat histories export in full too.
- **No more word-for-word repeats**: when you answered with just a sticker or a word or two, she sometimes sent her last line again; now she says something new.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
