# 2.5.3

She now looks things up on the web when you need it.

## Improvements

- **She looks it up when it matters**: when you ask about the weather, the news or whether something is true, she checks the web first and tells you in her own words; everyday chat and letters about daily life are not looked up. Only the part of your message that can be shared publicly is used for the lookup, never anything private between you.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
