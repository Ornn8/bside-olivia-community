# 2.5.17

She brings more of your own words when she recalls past conversations, can cover a song in the style you ask for, and sends a song she brings you straight to QQ.

## Improvements

- **Recalled past conversations are written more compactly**: when she remembers something you talked about before, each exchange is now a short dated line with the repeated labels removed, so the same space holds more of what was really said.
- **Covers can follow the style you ask for**: when you ask her in QQ to sing a song and mention how you want it (for example "a rock version" or "slower, piano only"), she follows your words; if you don't say, she chooses as before.
- **A song she brings you arrives in QQ as a file**: when she reaches out with a finished song, it is sent to QQ as an mp3 file like a cover she sang, and added to the music library and her memory. If it cannot be sent, you are told the reason and the error code.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
