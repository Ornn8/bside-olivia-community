# 2.5.9

She recognizes old letters you paste to her, and reads the words in photos of letters.

## Improvements

- **She recognizes old letters you paste to her**: paste a whole letter from your earlier history and she knows which letter it is, who wrote it to whom and roughly when, then carries on from there instead of treating it as unfamiliar text.
- **Photos of letters are read**: when you send a photo that is mostly writing, such as a handwritten letter, notes or a chat screenshot, she reads what it says instead of only describing a sheet of paper.
- **Undated old letters find their place in time**: imported letters without a date are placed by the dated letters around them, so her memories of them are less muddled.

## Fixes

- **Imported old letters count toward your days together**: after moving to a new install and importing weeks of old letters, the days you spent together are counted by the dates of those letters instead of staying at the very beginning. If a busy moment interrupts the reading of old letters, it retries a little later instead of stopping.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
