# 2.4.15

If you've been away for a few days, she writes to you; when you give her something, she answers.

## New

- **She misses you when you're away**: If you haven't talked to her for a few days, she writes you a letter in the evening, or sends a voice message on QQ, to ask how you've been. At most twice in a row, so she never keeps pestering you.
- **Your gifts get an answer**: After you give her a camera, a kitten or a new outfit, she writes you a few lines with a photo of herself right then.
- **A first letter for new friends**: When you first meet her, she sends a "Nice to meet you" letter with a photo; in English if your interface is English.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
