# 2.5.21

When a picture sent on QQ could not be seen, the app now fetches it once more, and if that still fails she says plainly that she could not see it instead of guessing.

## Improvements

- **She says so when she could not see a QQ picture**: if a picture sent on QQ could not be seen at the time, the app now keeps the reason, fetches the picture once more, and if it still fails she tells you in her reply that she could not see it, instead of guessing what is in it. A downloaded file that turns out not to be a picture is cleaned up automatically.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
