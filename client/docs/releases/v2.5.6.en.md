# 2.5.6

Photos from your phone attached to a letter now reach her.

## Fixes

- **Phone photos in letters reach her**: photos taken on an Android phone with "Ultra HDR", and high-resolution photos, can now be seen by her when attached to a letter.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
