# 2.5.1

Steadier starts, and small fixes for replies after a call and for letters.

## Improvements

- **One more click brings her back**: clicking the icon while Olivia is open now brings her window to the front (also out of the tray) instead of closing and reopening her.
- **No repeated repairs or restarts**: on computers that start Olivia as administrator, the permission repair runs only once; with UAC turned off, starts no longer switch back and forth; the nightly update restart waits for the repair and no longer reports a failure by mistake.
- **Opens even if she was not closed cleanly**: a window left over from an earlier session is ended before the start, so the new window no longer closes right away.
- **A busy port is named**: when another program holds the port Olivia starts on, a dialog says which program it is, and the start log records it.
- **Normal replies after a call**: after a phone call she no longer reads the call note back to you as her reply; what was said in the call is only background she knows.
- **No blank block in letters**: when a passage is removed from a letter, no empty block is left at the top.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
