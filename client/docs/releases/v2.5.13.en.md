# 2.5.13

Share a song in QQ and ask her to sing it, and she now really agrees and sings it; she also sends stickers in QQ again.

## New

- **Share a song, say you want to hear her sing it, and she agrees first, then sends it when it is ready**: send her a NetEase Cloud Music or Bilibili song card or link and say you want to hear her sing it. She first answers that she will sing it, the cover takes a while to make, and when it is done the mp3 file arrives in QQ. If it cannot be made, she tells you the specific reason and the error code right away. She does not need to have "heard" the song first.

## Improvements

- **She sends stickers in QQ again**: when the moment is light, shy, thankful or teasing, she adds a sticker after her words; she does not when you are talking about something serious or feeling down.
- **Just sharing a song does not make her start singing**: when you only share a song or praise it, she tells you she can sing it and asks if you would like to hear it.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
