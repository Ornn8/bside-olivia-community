# 2.5.27

The quality check on a reply no longer fails the whole letter on accounts with very long histories.

## Improvements

- **Replies**: on accounts with a very long conversation and memory, the quality check run after a reply is written was sometimes refused as too large, so the letter could not be sent. It now retries once with only the newer part of the record, and the letter goes out.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
