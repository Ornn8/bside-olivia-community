# 2.5.8

The QQ settings page shows the right state after login again.

## Fixes

- **The QQ settings page shows the right state after login**: once QQ is logged in, Settings → QQ now shows "Connected" instead of staying on the QR code or "QQ signed out", and no longer shows "state is not defined".

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
