# 2.4.8

Letters and memories in one place, cleaner letter deletion, and updates that wait for her reply.

## New

- **Letters and memories**: the "Letters and memories" button at the top brings managing letters and her memories together. Search the full text of all letters, select letters to delete, browse what she remembers, and jump from a memory to its letter. Recently deleted letters can still be restored. The long-term memory page in Settings opens it directly.

## Fixes

- **Cleaner deletion**: Deleting a letter also clears the memories and text that came from it, and restoring brings everything back. Deleting only her reply no longer removes memories that came from your letter.
- **Days with a diary**: Letters from a day with a diary entry can now be deleted, with the option to delete that day's diary and have it written again.
- **Nicknames**: The relationship panel shows only the names you call each other, not whole sentences.
- **Updates wait for her reply**: Restarting to update waits until she finishes the reply she is writing, and a reply cut off by a restart is written again and sent.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
