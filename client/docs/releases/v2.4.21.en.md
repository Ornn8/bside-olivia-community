# 2.4.21

You can now pick exactly which outfit she wears today, and the wardrobe opens faster.

## New

- **Pick her outfit**: outfits you own in the wardrobe now have "Wear this". She wears that outfit today, in her photos too. Choose a style again to let her pick her own outfits.

## Improvements

- **Wardrobe pictures**: the wardrobe page uses small compressed pictures, so it opens faster.
- **Her answer to a gift**: when you give her something, her answer goes out in the next pause of your chat instead of waiting until you stop writing. If QQ never pauses, it arrives as a letter.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
