# 2.5.23

On a slow connection she no longer wrongly says the cover service cannot be reached.

## Improvements

- **No more false "cover service unreachable" on a slow connection**: the client now remembers what the service offered and keeps using it after a restart, and waits longer when a song is waiting.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
