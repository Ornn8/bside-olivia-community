# 2.4.9

Missing diary entries are written, her mealtimes make more sense, and Settings is simpler.

## Fixes

- **Missing diary entries**: Diary entries from the past few days that could not be written are written automatically after the update (within the last week).
- **Mealtimes**: A meal missed while the computer was off is recorded as eaten as planned or skipped, and is no longer moved right next to the following meal. Breakfast starts by 10:30 and lunch by 16:30 at the latest.
- **Simpler Settings**: Long-term memory is viewed in "Letters and memories" at the top; Settings no longer repeats the entry.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
