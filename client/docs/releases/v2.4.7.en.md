# 2.4.7

Her diary arrives on time, and you can keep writing while she records or takes a photo.

## Fixes

- **No more missing diary entries**: with some models a day's diary could fail to be written. It is now written reliably, and entries missed in the last few days are written after the update.
- **Write while she creates**: while she is recording a voice reply, singing, or taking the photo you asked for, you can keep writing new letters. The same request is not started twice while it is still in progress.
- **Deleting letters**: letters that could not be deleted with a "can't be safely recalculated" message can now be deleted.
- **A steadier daily life**: her day no longer gets stuck on the same moment, and her plans get more natural names.
- **Naps end on time**: a nap that is due ends instead of being extended again and again.
- **More of the English interface**: the World page shows her activities, places and routine in English, and update notes come in English too.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
