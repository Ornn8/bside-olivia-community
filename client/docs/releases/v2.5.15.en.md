# 2.5.15

Asking her to sing a shared song is more dependable: one slow network answer no longer turns it off quietly, and she no longer promises things she is not going to do.

## Improvements

- **Covers no longer switch off silently after one slow network answer**: if a check for the cover ability was slow once, covers used to be treated as unavailable without a word. Now the check is repeated only when you actually share a song, so ordinary chat is not slowed down.
- **A shared song is kept and fetched on a later turn**: if a song link or card cannot be fetched right away, she keeps it and fetches it in a later turn instead of losing it.
- **When a cover cannot be made, she tells you the reason and the error code plainly**: she no longer acts as if nothing was asked.
- **No more promises for later without an order**: when she has not really started a song, photo or video, she no longer says she will send it later.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
