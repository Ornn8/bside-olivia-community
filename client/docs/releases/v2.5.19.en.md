# 2.5.19

She can sing back a voice message of you singing or humming, covers can follow the original arrangement, and a song that has just arrived is no longer blocked by an outdated refusal.

## Improvements

- **She can sing your song back to you**: in the QQ private chat, send her a voice message of you singing or humming (3 to 60 seconds). She decides for herself whether to sing the same piece back in her own voice, voice only with no accompaniment, or to decline in her own words. For a hum she writes words for the tune. The result comes back as an mp3 like a cover and is added to the music library; when it cannot be done you are told the reason and an error code once.
- **Covers can keep the original arrangement**: when you clearly ask for the original arrangement without changes, she keeps the song's own accompaniment and key and only the singer becomes her; when you do not, covers work as before.
- **A song that has arrived is no longer blocked by an old refusal**: if she once said she could not cover right now, and the song then arrived or covers were in fact possible, the old refusal no longer makes that cover count as invalid.

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
