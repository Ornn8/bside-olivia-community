# 2.5.18

Diaries keep their paragraphs, lost diary days get another try, and letter titles and songs are named right.

## Improvements

- **Diary entries are laid out in paragraphs**: an entry is now a few sentences together with one blank line between paragraphs, instead of a narrow column with one sentence per line. Entries already written are regrouped when you open them.
- **Diaries fail less often**: when the model writing the diary sent back a draft with a small formatting slip, it used to be unreadable; most of those drafts can now be used. If the same model fails twice on the same day, another model takes over.
- **Diary days that were lost get one more try**: days of the last week that could not be written because the draft was unreadable, and monthly memoirs lost the same way, are tried once more after you update (for a memoir, press the button once more under "Gather earlier memories").
- **Letters she starts keep their titles**: a title answer wrapped in a code block, with words around it, or split over two lines is now read; if there really is no title she asks once more instead of always using the same fixed title.
- **A song she brings is no longer marked as a cover**: the mp3 file sent to QQ and its name in the music library are the song's own name; only a cover she sang carries "(cover)".

## Updating

Version 2.4.0 and later update automatically: when "Update ready" appears at the top, you can read what's new and restart to update, or let it apply the next time you open the app.
